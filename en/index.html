Our mission

The digital world should be accessible to everyone. We focus on building tools optimized for screen readers and efficient keyboard navigation. We believe in clean code, openness, and software that serves people regardless of disability.

In development (Beta)

VoxRox Mail

A next-generation mail client that puts email back into the hands of blind and low-vision users. Instead of complex graphical interfaces, we offer speed and full compatibility with assistive technologies.

  • Standards: Full IMAP and SMTP protocol support.
  • Accessibility: Optimized for screen readers, verified on Windows with NVDA and JAWS.
  • Freedom: Open-source, with privacy at its core.

Under the hood

A native desktop application built on the Tauri framework with a powerful Java (Spring Boot) core running locally as a sidecar. Your mail stays on your device. We currently develop and test on Windows; macOS (VoiceOver) and Linux (Orca) versions are on the roadmap.
